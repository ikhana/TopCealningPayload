# 06. Google Business Profile copy

Final text to enter on the Google Business Profile. Written 2026-10-09 from what the website pages say,
and adjusted after a look at competitor vocabulary (their service names: house cleaning, deep cleaning,
move-in/move-out, Airbnb turnover, post-construction, commercial and office, recurring). Limits: service name 120
characters, service description 300, business description 750. No links, no offers, no dashes.

Google has no documented ranking weight for service descriptions. The service NAME is what searches are
matched against, so names use the phrases customers type. Do not stuff keywords into descriptions.
